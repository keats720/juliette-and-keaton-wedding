Accommodation
Hotel

There are a few great options for nearby accommodation. We recommend booking your stay at the Coast Bastion Hotel as we will be running a shuttle to the venue and back on the wedding day. If you plan to book here, please read these details, to ensure you get the best price.

There are also other hotels nearby that you could stay at and still use the shuttle.

Saturday Camping Option

For our outdoor enthusiasts, there will be an option to pitch a tent in a nearby field on Saturday night. Please note there will be no running water and no bathrooms. BYOT (Bring your own tent).

We will be sharing more details to those who RSVP for this option, including an arrival window to pitch your tent on the Saturday.

Getting There
A shuttle service will be provided on Saturday to and from the Coast Bastion Hotel. If you're staying elsewhere, there will be plenty of parking available on site.
